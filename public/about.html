Born from the desire to reimagine the classic charm bracelet into an architectural everyday bangle with silent gliding precision and limitless expression.

Solid 18K Recycled Gold

No Plating. No Hollow Tubing.

Our Dual-Track Glider Bangle is CNC-milled and hand-finished from solid recycled 18K gold or sterling silver—never plated brass. Each recessed channel measures precisely 0.85mm deep so solid gold charms lock smoothly without catching sweaters or hair.

Every diamond pavé charm uses ethically sourced G-H VS natural stones set under microscope, paired with genuine iridescent Mother of Pearl and hand-fired Italian enamel.
