1. Executive Summary

Niche Jewelry is a luxury atelier e-commerce platform built to offer personalized, interactive 3D fine jewelry customization. This document outlines the technical scope, timeline, and market benchmarks across four core functional tracks:

1. 3D Engine

In-house 3D modeling, WebGL rendering, real-time metal reflection, 360° orbit, and interactive drag-and-drop customization.

2. Web & Backend

Full-stack Next.js web application, PostgreSQL schema, 40+ REST API endpoints, and a comprehensive merchant administration panel.

3. Universal Payments

Worldwide Visa and Mastercard checkout with 3D Secure 2.0, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and automated invoicing.

4. KYC & Compliance

Tiered customer verification (SMS OTP, ID check) and full jewelry merchant compliance (conflict-free certification, hallmarking).

5. Section 4: KYC & Regulatory Compliance

Because jewelry is categorized as high-value goods, regulatory compliance and customer verification are critical to safeguard merchant accounts against fraud and chargebacks:

A. Customer Verification Tiers (To Maximize Conversion)

Verification Tier Order Threshold Verification Requirement Estimated Cost
Tier 0 (Guest) Under $150 Email confirmation only (charms, accessories) $0.00
Tier 1 (Registered) $150 – $1,000 Phone verification via SMS One-Time Password (OTP) Free via Firebase (or $0.05/SMS)
Tier 2 (High Value) $1,000 – $5,000 Government ID upload & verification (Stripe Identity / Sumsub) $1.35 – $1.50 per check
Tier 3 (VIP / Custom) Over $5,000 ID + proof of address + manual merchant confirmation $1.50 + manual review

B. Merchant Documents Required for Activation

  • Commercial Registration / Certificate of Incorporation: Official business registration certificate.
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN / VAT): Official tax registration certificate.
  • Authorized Manager ID: Government-issued passport or national identity card (both sides).
  • Corporate Bank Account Statement: Official business bank account statement (IBAN / Wire details).
  • Conflict-Free & Hallmarking Statement: Self-certified declaration published on the website confirming precious metal stampings (14K / 925).
